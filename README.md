# E-commerce Profit Leakage & Recovery Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes 1,000 e-commerce order records to identify where profit is leaking due to discounts, product returns, COD risk, shipping cost, city-level issues, and loss-making products.

The goal of this project is not only to show sales performance, but to identify business problems and suggest profit recovery actions.

## Business Problem
An e-commerce business may have good revenue and order volume, but profit can still remain low due to hidden leakage points such as:

- Heavy discounting
- Product returns
- COD-related risk
- High shipping cost
- Loss-making products
- City-level return issues

This project helps answer:

- Where is profit leaking?
- Which categories, cities, and products are risky?
- How much profit can be recovered through better decisions?

## Key Insights
- Discount leakage is one of the major reasons for reduced profitability.
- Product returns create direct profit loss.
- COD orders need monitoring because they may increase return and delivery risk.
- Some products generate low or negative net profit.
- Shipping cost reduces final net margin.
- Profit can be recovered by controlling discounts, reducing returns, optimizing shipping, and shifting COD users to prepaid/UPI.

## Business Recommendations
- Control unnecessary discounts on low-margin products.
- Reduce return rate through better quality checks and product descriptions.
- Encourage prepaid and UPI payments to reduce COD risk.
- Review or discontinue loss-making products.
- Optimize shipping cost and logistics strategy.
- Monitor high-risk cities and categories.
